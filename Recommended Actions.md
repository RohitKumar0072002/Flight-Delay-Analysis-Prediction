## Recommended Actions

**Action 1 — Break the Chain Reaction Problem:**
Late Aircraft causes 39% of all delays — not because one flight 
is late, but because that same plane is used for the next flight.
Fix: Airlines should maintain at least 1-2 "buffer aircraft" per 
hub that can replace delayed planes immediately, cutting chain 
reaction delays by estimated 25-30%.

**Action 2 — Stop Reacting to Peak Season Every Year:**
June and July cross 22% delay rate every single year — yet airlines 
still scramble for staff during these months.
Fix: Begin crew hiring and training by April, not June. Data clearly 
shows this is not unpredictable — it happens every summer without fail.

**Action 3 — Hold Budget Carriers Accountable with Data:**
Frontier (25.1%) and JetBlue (24.8%) are 6-7 percentage points 
above the industry average — consistently, year after year.
Fix: Introduce route-level delay scorecards tracked monthly. 
Routes crossing 25% delay rate for 3 consecutive months should 
trigger mandatory operational audit.
